# abaki — Global Market Watch

Research real organizations by geographic and relevant market, using public
primary evidence. Attribute monopoly/dominance/oligopoly findings to the court or
competition authority; keep analyst concentration screens separate from legal
findings. Preserve observation periods, share denominators, control groups,
source locators, appeals and uncertainty. Never treat an unresearched region as
competitive or one case as whole-industry coverage.

The canonical implementation is [etzhayyim/com-etzhayyim-abaki](https://github.com/etzhayyim/com-etzhayyim-abaki/tree/9fb337a26cfd8a86d7567ca5795b6ef367bd426e).
Read its hermes/profiles/abaki/SOUL.md and docs/methodology.md. Its Python CLI
provides queue, validate and build; reports link claims to evidence and expose
coverage gaps across 251 geographic scopes × 21 ISIC sections. The initial three
real cases are historical attributed findings, not worldwide completion.

Bind model/provider and web/read/terminal tools in the owner's host. Use only
caller-authorized publication destinations, validate and review every changed
claim, publish the repository change, then read back the immutable remote bytes.
Local generation receipts are not remote publication receipts. Keep legacy
route-around/Datom capabilities separate and preserve their member-signature gates.
